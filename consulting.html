I work with educators, clinicians, researchers, and scholarly teams to clarify difficult questions, strengthen projects, and translate ideas about evidence and clinical inquiry into usable forms.

Ways to work together

Focused help across clinical inquiry.

An engagement may be a single conversation, a review of work in progress, or a longer collaboration with an agreed scope and deliverables.

01

Scholarly inquiry

Research questions, study logic, quantitative interpretation, Bayesian reasoning, causal assumptions, and the movement from observations toward warranted claims.

02

Knowledge and reasoning

Mechanistic explanation, evidence integration, causal knowledge representation, clinical instantiation, and transparent systems for patient-specific reasoning.

03

Curriculum and faculty development

Curriculum mapping, course and module design, learning activities, assessment, and faculty development in scientific and clinical inquiry.

04

Mentoring

Thoughtful discussion for clinicians, educators, researchers, and emerging scholars developing a project, manuscript, curriculum, or longer-term scholarly direction.

Start with a conversation
