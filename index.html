The premise

Clinical reasoning needs infrastructure.

Scientific papers tell us about populations. Clinical practice asks what is likely true—and what to do next—for one person.

My work examines the space between those forms of knowledge. It brings together physical therapy, physiology, movement measurement, ergonomics and human factors, causal analysis, evidence synthesis, and education with an emerging program in computational clinical inquiry.

How the program fits together →

Movement Systems · HMS Lab

Physical therapists are human movement system specialists.

Movement Systems studies how movement emerges, how it can be measured, and how population knowledge about movement becomes useful for one person.

The Human Movement Systems Laboratory is the program’s clinically oriented empirical arm. It brings observations and questions from physical therapy into governed measurement, experimentation, mechanistic explanation, modeling, and renewed clinical inquiry.

Its work supports Plymouth State University DPT’s clinicians-first, hands-on learning philosophy and connection to the Pro Bono Clinic while keeping clinical care, education, and research distinct and appropriately governed.

Clinical Inquiry Ecosystem

Distinct forms of scholarship, connected.

Inspired by Boyer’s model of scholarship, five independent projects contribute different kinds of knowledge. Evidence and physiological and movement mechanisms inform integrative population models, which support—but never determine—reasoning about an individual patient.

Discovery scholarship

stats4PT

Develops the language and methods for moving from observations toward evidence, estimates, uncertainty, and warranted scientific or causal claims.

Generative mechanisms

Physiolog

Contributes physiological knowledge about how and why effects occur, adding causal depth beyond observed interventions and outcomes.

Movement knowledge

Movement Systems

Develops movement-domain theory, measurement, modeling, and empirical inquiry through the Human Movement Systems Laboratory.

Integrative scholarship

Models4PT

Integrates evidence, mechanisms, context, provenance, uncertainty, and disagreement into comprehensive population-level causal models.

Practice scholarship

Clinical Inference Engine

Combines population causal knowledge with individual information to support inspectable, patient-specific practice reasoning.

stats4PT evidence + Physiolog mechanisms + Movement Systems knowledge→Models4PT population causal knowledge→Clinical Inference Engine patient-specific instantiation
